# Modular Mini Figure Display Shelf

Creator: vrplanet. [Printables model 1179271](https://www.printables.com/model/1179271-modular-mini-figure-display-shelf). The archived maker PDF (local `1179271-modular-mini-figure-display-shelf-a220a9e8-1ded-490d-8716-4f5738e840a5.pdf`) records CC BY-NC-ND 4.0.

The maker recommends 0.2 mm layers, two walls, five top and five bottom layers, and 15% gyroid infill. Start with a top piece and add shelves via the dovetails. Test the joint with the clearance plate in the download before committing to a full build. The maker suggests a small rubber mallet for the snug joint; the joints need no glue or fasteners. Use the optional feet for a desktop build or the hidden screw holes for wall mounting.

The 3MF (local `modular-display-shelf.3mf`), three add-on shelves, three top styles, and foot STL remain together. Maker settings are from the archived PDF; local slicing and fit were not tested.

## Maker's notes

Paraphrased from the indexed Printables description, researched November 10, 2026:

- The refill coil mounts between two spool halves that snap together without tools.
- A solid, hole-free version is also offered for faster printing.
- The March 17, 2026 update revised Side B to prevent refill binding caused by a dimensional gap. The maker recommends replacing the old Side B; other files received only higher-resolution updates.

# Stroller umbrella holders

The maker package (local `Maker-package/`) is wallace's [Stroller Ubmrella Holder, Printables model 460943](https://www.printables.com/model/460943-stroller-ubmrella-holder). The archived maker PDF (local `Maker-package/460943-stroller-ubmrella-holder-7edd4b46-2b31-4e6b-9eab-d6ab086cebdc.pdf`) records CC BY-SA 4.0. It contains the original print, assembly, and customization instructions.

- Maker example: Elegoo black PLA, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.16 mm layers, 215 °C and 20% gyroid. Use three walls for the clamps; the rod example used four walls with solid infill at the thread.
- The maker used M3 × 8 mm screws with nuts and advises avoiding protruding screws. Tighten enough to stop slipping without cracking the clamps. The maker notes limited durability testing, especially for the PLA rod.
- The included OpenSCAD source uses BOSL2 and exposes clamp parameters at the top of the file.

The two related 3MFs (local `Related-3MF/`) are named `Version 3 Stroller Umbrella Holder (Fully Adjustable)` and its `updated` variant. Their listing and relationship to wallace's package are unverified, so the maker's instructions above apply only to the original package.

## Maker's assembly notes

Paraphrased from the [maker's Thangs listing](https://thangs.com/prints/designer/ApolloRoboto/3d-model/Magnetic%20Toggleable%20Physical%20Todo%20List%20For%20Fridge-1307376), researched November 10, 2026:

1. Paint the separated switch pieces with red and green high-gloss acrylic paint. Use the bed-facing surfaces for a smoother finish; allow them to dry before pressing the square insert into place.
2. Fit 6 × 2 mm magnets (the maker used TRYMAG) with a very small amount of cyanoacrylate glue. Extra magnets on the opposite side help hold them while drying.
3. Press-fit the case together.
4. Optionally coat the back with clear Plasti Dip to reduce sliding on the refrigerator.

The maker's V2 improves tolerances, angles slider corners, lengthens case walls and gives the AMS version a solid base. The local file's V2 status is unknown. No numeric slicing settings were recovered.

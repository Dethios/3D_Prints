## Maker's print and assembly notes

Paraphrased from the [maker's Thangs listing](https://thangs.com/designer/teol037/3d-model/Samsung%20Galaxy%20Watch%205%2F6%2F7%2FUltra%20Charging%20Stand%20%28Galaxy%20Watch%203%2F4%20support%29-1218044), researched November 10, 2026:

- Print as one piece without supports. The photographed example used black PolyLite ABS, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers, three perimeters and 23% infill.
- Watch 5 Pro/6/7/Ultra variants fit 29–30 mm charging pucks; Watch 3/4 variants fit 33–34 mm pucks. Check the actual local variant before printing.
- Bands wider than 24 mm can interfere when routed behind the stand. The puck is a tight push fit with an upper compliance cutout.
- Solid versions can use 0% infill and a print pause to add ballast. Another base variant accepts double-stacked M5 nuts.
- Tabbed variants have removable adhesion tabs; other variants may need a small brim.

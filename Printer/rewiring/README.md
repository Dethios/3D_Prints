## Related Printables listings

- fbeauKmi links separate A-drive mounts for the Voron 2.4 and Trident. For the Trident version, the maker specifies two M3 × 18 SHCS and one M3 5 × 4 heat-set insert. Install the insert in P1, seat the gland and cable, close the holders and secure the plate to the A-drive. The linked Trident design is GPLv3. [Voron 2.4 A-drive mount](https://www.printables.com/model/405358-a-drive-mount-for-pug-voron-24) · [Trident A-drive mount and instructions](https://www.printables.com/model/558536-a-drive-mount-for-pug-voron-trident)
- IMALlama's back-gantry mount lists `Gantry PUG pt1.stl` and `Gantry PUG pt2.stl`. Its published filenames differ from the local A/B pair. [Voron 2.4 back-gantry mount](https://www.printables.com/model/1086169-voron-24-back-gantry-pug-umbilical-mount)
- The Snigz H36 board STEP is listed separately. [H36 toolhead board source notes](../../Printables/h36-wwg2-a4t-pug/README.md)

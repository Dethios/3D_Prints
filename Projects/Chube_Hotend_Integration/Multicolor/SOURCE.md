# Chube Air UHF CW2/Tap hexagon cowling

The base cowling is the Chube adaptation described in the [project README](../README.md). The hexagon inlays come from [Armchair Heavy Industries’ A4T](https://github.com/Armchair-Heavy-Industries/A4T/tree/d675d3518c5a6213bb0d9dcc7380bbf9342066df), under `CAD/Cowlings [Hexagon multi-colour]/Hex A4T Cowling - Dragon-Rapido UHF [cw2-tap].step`.

The generator splits the modified Chube cowling at the inlay boundaries to make separate flush color bodies. The resulting cowling geometry, preview, and 3MFs retain the donor’s [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 terms](https://github.com/Armchair-Heavy-Industries/A4T/blob/d675d3518c5a6213bb0d9dcc7380bbf9342066df/LICENSE).

# Treasure crab Mickey ears for NX3

The ear faces are 99.8 mm wide and 4.75 mm thick at the highest relief. Each has an open ring, an inward-facing claw, a coin pile, a four-point star and a faceted jewel. The eyes are a separate magnetic unit. Left and right refer to the front view. The render is a paint guide for the single-material STLs and shows the approved NX3 placement on a representative band.

Use **26 magnets, each 6 mm in diameter × 3 mm thick**, for the complete set. Each ear uses five in the carrier and five in its band base. The eyes and their crown base use three each. Use one consistent polarity on the accessories and the opposite polarity on the band bases.

## Print and assemble

1. Import the STLs in their supplied orientation and at 100% scale. The faces and eyes print flat, relief up. The carriers and bases print with their flat sides on the bed. The checked Voron profile used a 0.5 mm nozzle, a 0.25 mm first layer and 0.15 mm layers. A 4 mm outer brim cleared the slicer's bed adhesion warnings on the narrow carriers, bases and test strip. Keep support material out of the horizontal magnet pockets.
2. Print one fit strip and one ear glue-on base first. Test the magnet fit, mating surfaces and placement on the actual headband before printing the full set. Remove the brim and any first-layer flare that interferes with seating.
3. Seat each face on the matching carrier using the two pegs and the sockets on the face's underside. The 3.2 mm pegs mate to 3.9 mm sockets. The joint allows a nominal 0.1 mm adhesive gap. The pegs locate the face while the broad surface takes the adhesive. Bond the face and carrier before wearing.
4. Dry-fit both ear bases approximately 40° to either side of the crown. Bond the three-pocket center base against the **front face of the crown** using its flat side. The art is oriented for these placements. The actual band determines contact area and final position.
5. Mark magnet polarity and secure the magnets. Let adhesive cure before bringing the mating parts together. Check seating and retention on the actual headband.

## Validation

All seven STLs reloaded as one watertight solid each. Both glue-on base STLs and STEP files are direct copies of the approved Sorcerer NX3 files. The ear carriers keep the five-pocket NX3 strip below the locating pegs. The eye unit keeps the three middle NX3 pockets. The nominal assembly check found zero intersections between the left, right and center assemblies and measured 391.5 mm² of projected bonding area per face. Physical magnet fit, bonding and retention remain unverified.

PrusaSlicer 2.9.7 sliced all seven files with VictorCustom and Polymaker PolyChroma Matte. Its CLI crashed on the two full saved print INIs, so the checks used scratch copies of the Cosmetic Parts and Functional Parts geometry settings. The toolpath sheet shows the first layers, selected relief layers and the magnet pocket regions. The original presets are outside this print kit.

See validation_report.json and slicer_preflight.json for the measured checks. The editable geometry is generated by cad/build_crab_nx3.py with the existing Sorcerer project as a sibling. Run it with Python 3.12 and the packages in that project's CAD/requirements.txt.

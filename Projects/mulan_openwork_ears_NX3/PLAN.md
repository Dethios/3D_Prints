# Build and verify the illustrated lithograph ears

1. Create illustrations with full costume edges. Fit the transparent images
   to their artwork fields and convert their tones into relief heights.
2. Join the relief to the backing and compare the reloaded STLs with the
   approved exterior, the stock NX3 strip and both nominal mount poses.
3. Compare coverage with the prior mesh. Review the source photos and new
   illustrations alongside the unpainted CAD and paint guide.
4. Slice the saved two-ear plate and review the artwork, ring, and pockets.

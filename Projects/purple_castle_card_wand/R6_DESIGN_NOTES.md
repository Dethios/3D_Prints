# R6 smaller head and enclosed card slot

The R6 prototype reduces the finished star from **140 to 130 mm** wide. The three flag contours clear the inner purple lip by 4.07 mm, 3.03 mm and 2.95 mm from left to right. The closest clearance to the gold rim is 5.49 mm. The deposited gold toolpaths reach all three flags.

R6 uses a fully enclosed portrait channel loaded through the bottom. With the full-size grip removed, a 53.98 × 85.60 × 1.20 mm gauge follows a straight path through the 55.00 × 1.80 mm slot. With the grip fully seated, its threaded end blocks the card and the cuff surrounds the two neck halves. Travel before contact is about 0.6 mm at the modeled seat. The rear shell is continuous. The front shell, rear shell and frame bond into one head, and the bow has broad fixed mounting lands. Retention uses the grip alone.

The split neck and cuff follow the local Minnie wand derivative of JC’s *Credit Card Wand (Flat Print)*, model 1636752, credited under Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial in the [mechanical source record](../minnie_ticket_wand/asa_cad/README.md). The interface stays at its original scale. The slot was widened to 1.80 mm for this revision.

R6 passed digital geometry and six local PrusaSlicer 2.9.7 K2 0.4 mm trial slices, including a slot-and-thread coupon. CAD establishes geometry and card blockage with the grip seated. Physical card fit, grip loosening, bonding, drop resistance and tap performance are pending. An adult should load and operate the grip. Keep the prototype away from the child until the physical checks are complete. The grip can still be unscrewed, so the design does not establish child resistance.

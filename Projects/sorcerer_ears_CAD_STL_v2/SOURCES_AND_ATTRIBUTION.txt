MODIFICATIONS IN THIS PACKAGE
- Original source meshes are retained in Reference_files with their authors
  credited above; descriptive local filenames identify the downloaded files.
- Functional lower mounting strips were clipped from the original ear files.
  A disconnected decorative shoe fragment in the NX3 crop was excluded.
- Source mounting strips were not scaled. Their curves, bore diameters,
  depths, spacing and axes were not replaced by guessed dimensions.
- A thin blue backing and the previously selected mouse/broom artwork were
  added above the functional mounting strips. Artwork was counter-rotated
  and fitted without altering the mounting interface.
- A 2 mm backing infill, inset 1.1 mm from the outline, closes the visual gap
  above the source bar. The source surfaces above the pocket-floor height
  and all original bores remain intact. Base-interference checks verify that
  this infill introduces no new interference with the source glue-on base.
- The NX3 glue base was rigidly rotated/translated into the ear's coordinate
  system. The Tim J glue base retains its original coordinates.
- Center hat strips/bases use the central three original magnet locations,
  with the outer two locations trimmed away. Their center placement is an
  adaptation for this project, not an original feature of the references.
- CAD STEP mounting surfaces are sewn faceted solids derived from the STL
  surfaces. The generator, source hashes and geometry report document this.
